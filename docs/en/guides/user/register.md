# Register and Create an Account

To purchase any of Virak Cloud's products, you first need to log into your account. If you haven't registered on the site yet, use this guide to familiarize yourself with the registration steps.

## Registration Steps ​

First, go to the [registration page](https://panel.virakcloud.com/user/register). Then, as shown in the image below, enter your first name, last name, and a valid email address in the designated fields, and choose a password (see [Password Requirements](#password-requirements) below). If you have a **referral code**, you can also enter it in the corresponding field; this field is optional and leaving it empty does not prevent registration.

## Password Requirements ​

Your password must meet the following requirements:

- At least one uppercase letter
- At least one lowercase letter
- At least one digit
- At least one special character (such as `!`, `@`, `#`, `$`)

If the entered password does not meet these requirements, the following message is shown:

::: warning Error Message
Your password must contain at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one special character, and one digit.
:::

## Accepting the Terms and Conditions ​

Before completing your registration, you must review and accept Virak Cloud's terms and conditions. Hovering over the terms checkbox shows the following hint:

::: tip Hint
Please, after reading the rules, if you approve, click on the "I accept the rules" button at the end of the rules.
:::

Clicking the terms and conditions link opens a window listing every section of the terms (privacy policy, user account, digital communications, third-party software licenses, pricing, VAT, force majeure, intellectual property rights, and the laws of the Islamic Republic of Iran).

::: warning Note
The "I accept the rules" button is located at the very bottom of this window and is not visible in the initial view; you need to scroll through the entire text to reach it.
:::

After reading and scrolling to the end, click the "I accept the rules" button. This activates the terms checkbox on the registration form.

## Email Confirmation ​

After submitting the form and accepting the terms, a confirmation email will be sent to you. Open the email and click the confirmation button. Your registration process will then be completed.
